# NHS CareerCoach AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 3, 2026

NHS CareerCoach AI is an independent educational career coaching app for UK healthcare and NHS job applicants. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the NHS.

## Data handled by the app

The submitted app is local-first. Profile choices, job applications, supporting statement drafts, STAR answers, mock interview notes, achievements, and voice transcripts are stored locally on your device using Apple local storage.

The AI-style coaching responses in this build are generated by the app's local coaching service. Job descriptions, supporting statement notes, application details, interview answers, STAR examples, and voice transcripts are not sent to a developer-operated server and are not shared with a third-party AI provider.

Speech-to-text uses Apple's system speech recognition permission when you choose to use voice features. Apple may process speech recognition requests according to Apple's own privacy terms and device settings.

## Developer collection

The app does not require an account and does not collect personal data on developer-operated servers as part of normal app use.

If you contact support by email, GitHub, App Store review, or another external channel, the information you choose to provide may be used only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, and improve the app.

## Purchases

This submitted build does not sell digital purchases.

## Data export and deletion

The app includes local data export and local delete controls. Deleting local app data removes locally stored coaching and application information from the device, subject to normal iOS backup and system behavior.

## Educational disclaimer

NHS CareerCoach AI provides educational coaching, drafting support, interview preparation, and career planning prompts. It does not guarantee interviews, job offers, promotion, progression, or employment outcomes. AI suggestions should be reviewed, personalised, and checked for accuracy before submission.
