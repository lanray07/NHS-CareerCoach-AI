# NHS CareerCoach AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 27, 2026

NHS CareerCoach AI is an independent educational career coaching app for UK healthcare and NHS job applicants. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the NHS.

## Data handled by the app

The app is designed to work local-first. Profile choices, job applications, supporting statement drafts, STAR answers, mock interview notes, achievements, and voice transcripts are stored locally on your device using Apple local storage.

Mock AI coaching is enabled by default. In this mode, your job descriptions, notes, statements, interview answers, and transcripts are processed on your device and are not sent to a developer-operated AI service.

Speech-to-text uses Apple speech recognition APIs when you choose to use voice features. Apple may process speech recognition requests according to Apple's own privacy terms and system settings.

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. The developer does not receive your full payment card details.

## Developer collection

The current app does not require an account and does not collect personal data on developer-operated servers as part of normal app use.

If you contact support by email, GitHub, App Store review, or another external channel, the information you choose to provide may be used only to respond to your request, troubleshoot issues, and improve the app.

## Optional future AI backend

The app includes placeholder architecture for a secure remote AI backend. No API keys are stored in the app. If a remote AI backend is enabled in a future version, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before release to explain what is sent, why, and how it is protected.

## Data export and deletion

The app includes local data export and local delete controls. Deleting local app data removes locally stored coaching and application information from the device, subject to normal iOS backup and system behavior.

## Educational disclaimer

NHS CareerCoach AI provides educational coaching, drafting support, interview preparation, and career planning prompts. It does not guarantee interviews, job offers, promotion, progression, or employment outcomes. AI suggestions should be reviewed, personalised, and checked for accuracy before submission.
